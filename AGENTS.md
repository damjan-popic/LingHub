# KLIKai language-tool repair method

Standing user guidance, 18 October 2026: KLIKai is a language tool, not merely
a lexicon. Repair the user's language task and the underlying construction or
scenario. Sources and analyses should support a practical answer, not replace
it with a dictionary inventory or a report of competing analyzers.

Before changing a linguistic rule or interpretation:

- Inspect the relevant full paradigm and related lexical classes. A reported
  example is a regression case, not the boundary of the repair. For masculine
  names/nouns in -a, consider Luka, Miha, Aljoša, vojvoda and independent cases,
  preserving source-specific variants, homographs and counterexamples.
- Check forms across constructions and contexts, including different word
  order, genuine ambiguity and cases where the rule does not apply. Separate
  inflection, syntax, meaning, register and normative judgments.
- Preserve complete source identities and paradigms before selecting evidence
  relevant to the question. Do not infer a preference from counts or generalize
  one lexeme's alternatives to every similar spelling.
- Keep request interpretation, retrieval, answer and source cards aligned.
  Generalize the mechanism rather than hardcode a word, source ID or answer.
- Evaluate whether the output helps the user make the requested language
  choice. Expected-word checks and dictionary facts alone are insufficient.
- Ask when meaning, context, audience or a product decision materially affects
  the repair. Preserve existing external-client defaults and service policies.
